01 What sports surfaces do you construct?
+

We construct FIFA-standard football turfs, box cricket grounds, official acrylic pickleball courts, multi-sport synthetic flooring, and running tracks.

02 How long does a turf or court construction take?
+

Typically, a standard 7-a-side football turf or pickleball court construction takes between 45 to 60 days depending on civil work requirements.

03 What sub-base preparation is required for turf?
+

We execute engineered crushed stone, PCC, or asphalt sub-bases with slope-based drainage channels to prevent waterlogging.

04 What materials are used for Pickleball Courts?
+

We install 5-layer and 8-layer ITF-certified acrylic cushion systems over concrete or asphalt for optimum ball bounce and joint protection.

05 Do you handle floodlighting and perimeter netting?
+

Yes, we provide full turnkey civil work including heavy-duty GI fencing poles, UV-stabilized nylon perimeter nets, and high-lumen LED floodlights.

06 What is the expected lifespan of synthetic turf?
+

Our high-density UV-stabilized synthetic turfs have an operational lifespan of 8 to 10+ years with minimal maintenance.

07 Can you construct courts for private residential spaces?
+

Yes! We construct custom mini-turfs, backyard pickleball courts, and multi-sport flooring for private villas, farmhouses, and gated communities.

08 Do you offer post-installation warranty & support?
−

Yes, all our construction projects come with a comprehensive manufacturer warranty on turf/surface materials and dedicated maintenance guidance.
